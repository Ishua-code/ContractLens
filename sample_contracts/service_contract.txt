SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Orion Facilities Services Ltd ("Service Provider") and Meridian Manufacturing Inc ("Client"), effective September 1, 2026.

Clause 1. Scope of Services
1.1 Service Provider shall perform quarterly equipment maintenance and safety inspections at Client's manufacturing facility.

Clause 2. Term
2.1 This Agreement shall commence on September 1, 2026 and continue for a period of 6 months, expiring on February 28, 2027.

Clause 3. Deliverable Deadline
3.1 Service Provider shall submit the Q3 Safety Inspection Report to Client no later than October 3, 2026.
3.2 Failure to submit the report by the deadline in Clause 3.1 shall be considered a material breach.

Clause 4. Payment Terms
4.1 Client shall pay Service Provider a fixed fee of INR 4,50,000 per quarter, payable within 15 days of invoice.

Clause 5. Obligations of Service Provider
5.1 Service Provider shall ensure all technicians are certified and follow Client's on-site safety protocols.

Clause 6. Obligations of Client
6.1 Client shall provide Service Provider with safe and timely access to all equipment requiring inspection.

Clause 7. Termination
7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice if the other party breaches a material obligation and fails to cure within 15 days.

Clause 8. Liability
8.1 Service Provider's liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid in the preceding quarter.

Clause 9. Governing Law
9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws applicable in the jurisdiction of Client's principal place of business.
