SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT (VERSION 1)

This SaaS Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Nimbus Cloud Pvt Ltd ("Provider") and Zenith Retail Ltd ("Customer"), effective January 1, 2026.

Clause 1. Definitions
1.1 "Services" means the cloud-based inventory management platform provided by Nimbus Cloud Pvt Ltd.
1.2 "Effective Date" means January 1, 2026.

Clause 2. Term
2.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of 12 months, expiring on December 31, 2026.

Clause 3. Service Level Obligations
3.1 Provider shall maintain a minimum of 99.5% uptime for the Services, measured monthly.
3.2 In the event uptime falls below 99.5% in any calendar month, Customer shall be entitled to service credits as described in Schedule A.

Clause 4. Renewal
4.1 This Agreement will automatically renew for successive 12-month terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal.
4.2 Notice of non-renewal must be provided at least 60 days before the end of the then-current term.

Clause 5. Payment Terms
5.1 Customer shall pay all invoices within 30 days of the invoice date (Net-30).
5.2 Customer's first invoice payment is due by February 1, 2026.
5.3 Late payments shall incur a late fee of 5% of the outstanding invoice amount per month.

Clause 6. Confidentiality
6.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party during the term of this Agreement.

Clause 7. Data Protection
7.1 Provider shall implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect Customer data.

Clause 8. Limitation of Liability
8.1 Provider's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the one (1) month preceding the claim.

Clause 9. Termination
9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing 90 days written notice to the other party.
9.2 Provider may terminate immediately if Customer fails to pay any invoice within 60 days of its due date.

Clause 10. Governing Law
10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws applicable in the jurisdiction of the Provider's principal place of business.

Clause 11. Liability Cap
11.1 Notwithstanding Clause 8, in no event shall Provider's aggregate liability exceed one (1) month of fees paid by Customer, regardless of the nature of the claim.
